# Mzansi Collab visual and video content plan

**Status:** IN PROGRESS

## Current original assets

- `public/images/mzansi-creators-studio.png` — original AI-generated editorial image used on `/creators`.
- `public/images/mzansi-business-collaboration.png` — original AI-generated editorial image used on `/business`.

These assets depict fictional adult creators and business collaborators. They contain no third-party logos, names or campaign material.

## Website direction

The public site should combine product information with human stories:

- Diverse, people-led visual storytelling across creator disciplines.
- Partner brand marks only after documented permission to display them.
- Distinct creator and business journeys rather than a single long marketing page.
- Short-form videos used as campaign stories, product explainers, creator interviews and practical education.

## Short-form video specification

Create 8–20 second silent-loop hero clips in 16:9 and 9:16 variants. Add subtitles or spoken narration only after a script and accessibility review. Each delivery should include:

- A compressed WebM or MP4 source for the website.
- A still poster image and concise descriptive alt text.
- Captions for any spoken dialogue.
- A rights/provenance record, including the generation platform and final approval.

## YouTube integration

The creator and business pages include intentionally labelled YouTube-ready showcases. Replace these placeholders only with Mzansi Collab-owned or licensed YouTube videos. The eventual content model should store a title, YouTube video ID, thumbnail, transcript/captions reference, audience, placement and publication state.

## Next content inputs

Before publishing actual partner or campaign content, collect:

1. Approved partner brand logos and display permission.
2. Mzansi Collab YouTube channel/video URLs or final embed IDs.
3. Approved scripts for the first creator and business clips.
4. Confirmation of the preferred external AI-video workflow and rights terms.
